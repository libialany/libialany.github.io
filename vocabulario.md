### Palabras a conocer

- ixVolumes are containerized storage volumes accessible only to the app

- A stripe in TrueNAS combines multiple hard drives into a single volume with no data protection.

- Spare: is an idle drive added to a storage pool. It automatically replaces a failed disk and begins rebuilding data via resilvering, minimizing downtime when you cannot swap a drive right away.

- LZ4 is a lossless data compression algorithm focused strictly on maximizing compression and decompression speed rather than achieving the highest compression ratio.
